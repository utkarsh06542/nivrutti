How-to guides

Use a reverse mortgage

  1. Homeowners over 60 can get monthly payments from a bank against their house while continuing to live in it.
  2. The payments are tax-free. The loan is repaid by selling the house after both spouses die, or your children can repay it and keep the house.
  3. Ask SBI and other large banks for their terms; compare the amount offered.
