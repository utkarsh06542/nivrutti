How-to guides

Build a safe FD ladder

  1. Keep no more than ₹5 L in any one bank in your name. Deposit insurance covers principal and interest up to that amount per bank.
  2. Split the money into FDs maturing in 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 years. When one matures, spend it or re-invest for five years.
  3. Choose monthly or quarterly interest payout.
  4. Small finance banks pay more; use them, but stay within ₹5 L each.
