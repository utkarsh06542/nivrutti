Open a Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS) account

  1. Go to any post office or a bank that offers SCSS (most large public and private banks do).
  2. Carry: account opening form, PAN, Aadhaar, a photo, age proof, and — if you are 55 to 60 — proof of your retirement benefit date.
  3. Deposit up to ₹30 L per person by cheque. A couple can hold up to ₹60 L across two accounts.
  4. Link your savings account so interest is credited on the first working day of April, July, October and January.
  5. Add a nominee. After five years you can extend in blocks of three years.

Interest is taxable. Under the old tax regime, the deposit counts for the ₹1.5 L deduction in the year you open it.
