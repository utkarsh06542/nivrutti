How the plan is built

Ideas that work in other countries, adapted to Indian schemes, taxes and families.

Buckets by time

Harold Evensky, United States

Money needed soon stays safe; money for later can grow. With three years of bills secured, a market fall never forces a sale.

A floor, then upside

Safety-first school, Wade Pfau

Essential spending is covered by income that doesn't depend on markets. Only the surplus takes market risk.

Three-part risk profile

SEBI suitability rules; MiFID, Europe

How you feel about losses, how much loss your finances can absorb, and how much return your plan needs are measured separately. The plan follows the safer of the first two and tells you when the third disagrees.

Guardrails on spending

Guyton and Klinger, United States

Small, rule-based cuts after bad years raise the odds of money lasting far more than a fixed "safe" percentage.

Lifetime income pooling

CPF LIFE, Singapore

Living to 95 is a risk to insure. The planner suggests a joint-life annuity when savings are stretched, sized so you keep control of the rest.

The survivor test

Pension practice in the UK and Netherlands

Plans are tested for the years after one spouse dies, when pension often falls by half or more but costs do not.

Spending that changes with age

David Blanchett's "retirement spending smile"

Travel and lifestyle spending ease after 75, while medical and care costs rise faster than prices.

Testing many futures

Monte Carlo planning

One average return hides the danger of a crash early in retirement. We simulate 1,000 paths with Indian return and inflation assumptions.

Scam warnings built in

ScamSmart, FCA United Kingdom

Retirees are the main target of fraud. The guides name the traps and how to report them.
